Roux Sports shows NHL scores, standings and team pages without ads, tracking or an account.

Scores: every game for the day, with live state, period-by-period scoring, shots, team stats, penalties and three stars. Swipe between days, or jump to any date from the month picker.

Standings: Eastern, Western and league tables, with playoffs, division and conference views. Conference and ranking choices are remembered between launches.

Teams: all 32 clubs, each with a full-season schedule centred on the next game, the roster, and league-relative team stats where every number carries its rank across the league.

Players: skaters and goalies with their season totals, and a player page with career and season history.

Game alerts: choose the teams you care about and get a notification when a game starts or its score changes. Alerts are off by default, need no account, and are scheduled on the device.

No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No third-party SDKs that phone home.
