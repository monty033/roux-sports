# Brief — restyle the scoring summary to match the reference format

Branch `task/gd5-scoring`, worktree `.worktrees/gd5-scoring`. PR #100 is already open on this branch; commit your work
here and do **not** push, do not merge, do not open anything.

## Why

Patrick supplied a screenshot from another hockey app and said: *"I like this format so let's try to match it as close
as possible."* The card shipped in `9d97a2e` has the right data and the right order; what changes is the row anatomy and
the period headings. His two decisions on the ambiguous parts: the number after the scorer's name stays our **season
goal count** (not their jersey number), and our card title stays at **14 sp** — match on layout only, do not resize
titles or touch other cards.

## What the reference does, mapped to us

A goal row is three regions, left to right:

1. **Club crest at the far left of the row** — the scoring club's crest, roughly 44 dp, left-aligned at the card's
   content edge. This replaces the crest that currently sits immediately left of the scorer's name; delete that
   placement rather than keeping both.
2. **Text column**, two or more lines:
   - line 1: the goal time, single bold/semibold run, primary colour, exactly the feed string (`12:49`, `18:26`);
   - line 2 onward: `Full Name <seasonGoals>` then, when applicable, the strength in prose in parentheses
     (`(power play)`, `(short-handed)`, `(empty net)`, `(penalty shot)`), then the assists as one inline sentence
     `(Assists: Noah Cates 3, Porter Martone 4)`.
   - Full names, not initials: the payload carries `firstName.default` and `lastName.default` for the scorer and for
     every assist, so compose `First Last` in the adapter and let the UI print it verbatim. Do not print `M. Michkov`.
   - The whole sentence wraps naturally and the wrapped lines stay aligned with the text column, not under the crest.
   - An unassisted goal prints no `Assists:` fragment at all — no parentheses with nothing in them, no em dash.
3. **Two score columns at the right edge**, away then home, each wide enough at font scale 2.0 not to clip, and aligned
   vertically with the **time line**, not with the middle of the text block.

**Emphasis:** the score that just changed is bold in the primary colour; the other side stays regular and muted. This is
per row: after an away goal the away number is the emphasised one, after a home goal the home number is.

**Period headings carry the clubs.** `1st` (bold, primary) sits at the left of the heading line, and the two club
abbreviations sit at the right, one above each score column, in the muted label style. That means the single header row
currently drawn above the first period goes away — the clubs are named on every period heading instead, which is what
the reference does. Keep the existing rule that a header is not a goal: nothing about headings may enter row indices or
the empty-card check.

**A thin muted divider** separates consecutive period blocks (inset, not full-bleed). One rule between blocks, none
above the first heading and none below the last row.

Everything else stays: card position (after shots, before game stats), period order ascending, elapsed-time ordering and
the verbatim clock, the card tag `detail.section.scoring-summary`, hide-when-empty, and the 48 dp reach floor for
anything interactive (nothing in this card is).

## Constraints

- `:domain` gains no framework types and no formatting helpers; the adapter composes the display strings.
- Type floors still apply: nothing below 12 sp, and the goal sentence is prose — 14 sp minimum, at font scale 2.0 too.
- Do not touch the period table, shots, stats, hero, scores, standings, or any other card. Their goldens must not change;
  if one does, that is a defect in this change.
- Audit fixtures must satisfy their own arithmetic: within a period the running score never falls and the elapsed clock
  never falls, and the last row's score equals the hero score for that state. Update the fixtures' player names to full
  names so the recorded image shows what the phone will.

## Tests, and the mutation that must fail for each

- Row anatomy: crest at the row's left edge is the scoring club's; the time line precedes the name line; the score
  columns sit right of the text column. Mutation: move the crest beside the name, or put the time on the same line as
  the scorer.
- Changed-score emphasis: after an away goal the away column carries the emphasised tag and the home does not, and the
  reverse for a home goal. Mutation: always emphasise home, or emphasise both.
- Full names: a row renders `Matvei Michkov`, never `M. Michkov`. Mutation: print `name.default` instead of first+last.
- Strength in prose: a power-play goal reads `(power play)`, an empty-netter `(empty net)`; no `PP`/`EN` codes remain.
  Mutation: revert to the abbreviations.
- Unassisted goal prints no `Assists:` text. Mutation: emit an empty parenthetical or a bare `Assists:`.
- Period heading carries both club abbreviations above their columns, and no single header row exists above the first
  period. Mutation: restore the global header, or drop one club from the heading.
- Divider count equals period blocks minus one. Mutation: draw a divider above the first block.
- The card still fits at 411 dp and 360 dp, font scale 1.0/1.3/2.0 without clipping a score column or the abbreviations.
